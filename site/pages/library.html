Resource library

The short version of each campaign lives on its page. This is where the record behind the work lives: the documents, the history and the coverage.

What qualifies: a citable record, such as a filing, report, comment letter, ordinance or dataset, or a campaign publication such as a newsletter or flyer, that names its source and is not the main narrative of a campaign page. Entries carry a date where the document carries one. Drafts and unowned material do not go here.
